MemoVR Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-25 · Contact: memovr180@gmail.com

English 中文

MemoVR Player does not upload your media, browsing history, or SMB credentials to the developer’s servers. The current build includes no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

1. What we collect

Data is processed only on your device and on LAN devices you choose to connect to:

DataStoragePurpose
Local / USB mediaYour storageBrowse, play, favorite, delete — not uploaded
Favorites & settingsOn-device app storageLocal preferences
SMB host, username, passwordOn-device SharedPreferencesReconnect to your LAN shares; not sent to our cloud
Thumbnail / temp cachesApp cacheFaster browsing

2. How we use data

  • Play and browse user-selected local, USB, or SMB videos/photos (including .osv, MP4, JPEG, PNG).
  • With your grant: browse folders, open non-MediaStore files, delete items you choose.
  • SMB credentials you enter stay on-device for later sign-in.
  • A short-lived HTTP helper may run on 127.0.0.1 for streaming playback (cleartext allowed for loopback only). Traffic does not leave the device for developer servers.

3. Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal data and do not send your media or SMB credentials to advertisers or analytics vendors. Meta/Android may process system-level data under their own policies.

4. Retention and deletion

Uninstall the app or clear app storage to remove preferences, cached SMB credentials, and caches. Original files on device or SMB remain yours unless you delete them in the app. Support: memovr180@gmail.com.

5. Children’s privacy

The app is for a general audience and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Permissions (summary)

  • All files access — browse any folder, open .osv and other non-MediaStore paths, play, and delete user-selected files.
  • Internet / Wi‑Fi — LAN SMB and on-device loopback helpers.
  • Legacy storage (API-limited) — older Android local file access only.

7. Changes

Material updates will revise the effective date on this page.

MemoVR Player · Privacy Policy (EN)

MemoVR Player 不会将您的媒体、浏览记录或 SMB 凭据上传到开发者服务器。当前版本未集成广告、统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK。

1. 我们收集什么

数据仅在您的设备及您主动连接的局域网设备上处理：

数据存放用途
本地 / USB 媒体您的存储浏览、播放、收藏、删除；不上传
收藏与设置本机应用数据本地偏好
SMB 主机、用户名、密码本机 SharedPreferences再次连接局域网共享；不发往我们的云端
缩略图等缓存应用缓存加快浏览

2. 如何使用

  • 播放与浏览您选择的本地、USB 或 SMB 视频/照片（含 .osv、MP4、JPEG、PNG）。
  • 在您授权下：浏览任意目录、打开非 MediaStore 文件、删除所选项目。
  • 您输入的 SMB 凭据仅保存在本机以便再次登录。
  • 播放部分流式内容时，可能在本机 127.0.0.1 运行短时 HTTP 辅助（故允许明文环回流量）。该流量不离开本机发往开发者服务器。

3. 共享与出售

我们不出售个人数据，也不将媒体或 SMB 凭据发送给广告商或分析服务。Meta/Android 可能按其政策处理系统级数据。

4. 保留与删除

卸载应用或清除应用数据即可删除偏好、SMB 凭据缓存与应用缓存。设备或 SMB 上的原始文件由您管理，除非您在应用内删除。支持邮箱：memovr180@gmail.com。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用面向一般用户，不专门收集 13 岁以下儿童的信息。

6. 权限摘要

  • 所有文件访问 — 任意目录浏览、打开 .osv 等非媒体库路径、播放与删除所选文件。
  • 网络 / Wi‑Fi — 局域网 SMB 与本机环回辅助。
  • 旧版存储权限（受 API 限制）— 仅较低 Android 版本本地访问。

7. 变更

重大更新将修订本页生效日期。
